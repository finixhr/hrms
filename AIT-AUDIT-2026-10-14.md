## C. Calculation-flow documentation

```

Employee.taxTin ─┐             SalaryStructure ─┐          PayrollRun (period)
taxCategory      ├─ profile    basic / gross    ├─ gross    gross, OT, bonus,
declaredInvest.  │             (ACTIVE, latest) │          commission, arrears
taxNetWealth     │                             │          (entries of PAID runs
taxMotorVehicles │                             │           inside the FY window)
previousEmployerIncome, taxAlreadyDeducted     │
                                                ▼
        ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
        │ §86 ESTIMATED TOTAL INCOME (payroll, per employee/month)     │
        │   YTD earnings already PAID this income year                 │
        │ + this period's payment (incl. OT/bonus/commission/arrears)  │
        │ + remaining months × regular monthly gross                   │
        │ + remaining festival-bonus projection (config count × basic   │
        │   MINUS bonuses already paid/paying)                          │
        │ + previous-employer income          [Employee.annualGross-   │
        │                                        Override wins if set] │
        └──────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┘
                                       ▼
   calculateAit(config[IY], input)   — the seven statutory steps
   1  annual income from employment (§32)
   2  − Third-Schedule exemption: MIN(⅓ income, ৳5,00,000) → taxable income
   3  − category threshold (annual FA table; senior auto from DOB, female
      auto from gender, explicit choice wins; non-resident → flat 30% path)
   4  progressive slabs on the excess (per-year widths/rates)
   5  §78 rebate: lower of 3%×taxable / rate×declared investment / cap
   6  minimum-tax floor test AFTER rebate (warning-only for TDS;
      minimumTaxEffective surfaces the ৳5,000 / ৳1,000 filing floor)
   7  surcharge on the tax after rebate (net-wealth bands + motor-car
      trigger; warning when net wealth not on record)
   8  §86(1) monthlyTds = this month's payment × average rate
      §86(4) monthlyTdsCatchUp = (annualTax − TDS actually withheld) ÷
      months remaining in the income year  ←  what payroll posts
                                       ▼
   PayrollEntry.ait (+ .aitPaisa) · breakdownJson.ait · AitCalculationLog
   (kind=PAYROLL, runId, inputsJson, configJson snapshot, outputJson)
                                       ▼
   PAY (final approval) → PAID runs become the actual-TDS base for the
   next month's §86(4) adjustment — the cycle is self-correcting
                                       ▼
   AIT MODULE: calculator (live preview) · batch (per branch) ·
   reconciliation (estimate vs PAID-run TDS, gap + catch-up months) ·
   TDS certificates (per month / per year, ESS self-service) ·
   audit journal browser — all read the SAME engine + SAME actuals
```

Rounding policy: money rounds to whole taka only at presentation/posting
(slab lines and totals); internal arithmetic keeps full precision; paisa
integer shadows are dual-written on payroll entries (H-09).

## D. Corrected implementation — what changed

**Engines** — `src/hrm-server/ait-engine.ts` (authoritative, rewritten core):
three-limb §78 rebate · post-rebate minimum tax with corrected citations ·
surcharge bands + triggers · non-resident flat rate · female auto-suggest ·
§86(1) and §86(4) outputs · two verified year presets · `ensureAitConfig`
creates BOTH years and activates the one containing today · config snapshot
extended. `src/hrm/shared/ait-engine.ts` (client mirror): same rebate/min-tax
math, corrected presets/AY, refreshed disclaimers.

**Payroll** — `src/hrm-server/payroll.ts`: FY-window actuals prefetch (one
query per data class), §86 estimate construction, catch-up posting, per-entry
journaling. `src/hrm-server/ait.ts` (`computeAit`): estimate/actuals/months
inputs, catch-up as `netMonthlyAit`, warnings passthrough.

**Routes** — `src/hrm-server/routes/ait.ts`: new config fields on save/seed;
`fyActuals`/`fyActualsBatch` (actual TDS base + months remaining) used by
calculator, batch, reconciliation and certificates; employee profile exposes
and persists netWealth/motorVehicles/previousEmployerIncome; display lines for
rebate limbs, minimum-tax floor, surcharge, §86(4) catch-up.

**UI** — `src/hrm/views/ait.tsx`: calculator inputs for net worth / motor
vehicles; corrected tooltips and citations everywhere (§78 limbs, §163
minimum tax, §18(2) slabs); fiscal-year dialog fields for the new limbs.

**Schema/migration 11** (`11_ait_legal_audit_20261014`): `AitFiscalYear.
rebateIncomeCapPercent` (default 3), `newTaxpayerMinimumTax` (nullable),
`surchargeJson` (nullable band table); `Employee.taxNetWealth`, `taxMotorVehicles`
(default 0); backfills the §78(c) cap ৳1 crore → ৳10 lakh on legacy rows. All
additive/nullable — existing rows and every historical `AitCalculationLog` row
are untouched and remain reproducible from their own snapshots.

**Seed** — both year configs (active-by-date), demo tax profiles
(investment rebate, surcharge triggers, disabled/FF categories), regenerated
payroll corpus.

## G. Outstanding issues and honest limitations

1. **Primary-source gaps.** The NBR website serves the ITA 2023 as a
   consolidated PDF dated 16 June 2025 (with FA 2024 footnotes); the Finance
   Ordinance 2025 / Finance Act 2026 **texts themselves** are not published
   as machine-readable official PDFs we could retrieve. Their figures were
   verified through KPMG's salient-features document and PwC's current-year
   table (both professional-firm publications of the enacted law) plus
   multiple independent practitioner sources that agree. **Recommendation:**
   have your tax counsel confirm the two year-tables against the official
   Gazette before production go-live; the admin UI (AIT → Fiscal Year Config)
   makes that a two-minute review per year.
2. **Threshold history.** For IY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26) the audit verified the
   figures (350k/400k/475k/500k, 5% first band, rebate 3%/15%/10L) but r8
   ships presets only for IY 2025-26 and IY 2026-27. A 2024-25 preset can be
   added in the same admin screen if historical re-computation is needed.
3. **Minimum-tax exceptions.** No salary-only/women/senior exemptions from the
   individual ৳5,000 floor were found in the ITA-era sources reviewed (the
   old ITO 1984 exceptions appear abolished; current guidance states a flat
   ৳5,000 for everyone above the threshold). Flagged for counsel confirmation
   in §G.1's review.
4. **Non-employment income.** Deliberately out of §86 withholding scope
   (documented + tested). The annual return computation for other income,
   final-tax items and foreign tax relief remains an NBR return-filing matter
   and is surfaced only through disclaimers.
5. **Surcharge house-size trigger** (>8,000 sft city-corporation house) needs
   a data input the HRM does not hold; it is called out in the warning text
   whenever net wealth is declared but the trigger cannot be fully tested.
6. **The §86(4) catch-up posts the full deficit spread over remaining
   months.** For an employee leaving mid-year the final month absorbs the
   whole remaining liability (statutorily correct; the existing M-5 net-pay
   clamp + warning prevents silent net-wipe). A "spread-cap" knob can be
   added if HR prefers softer catch-ups — noted as a product decision, not a
   compliance gap.
7. **Compliance conclusion.** Every rule implemented is now verified against
   the sources in §B and the mandated test matrix passes, but **100% legal
   compliance is NOT claimed**: items 1–3 above require official-Gazette and
   counsel confirmation that this audit could not perform from here.
