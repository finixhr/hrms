## 1) Loan & Advance deducted from salary — with GUARANTOR fallback + a control system under Payroll

**The rule (as requested):**
- The monthly loan installment / advance recovery deducts from the
  **borrower's** salary, exactly as before.
- When the borrower has **no payable salary in the period** (absent the whole
  period, or off the payroll entirely), the due installment moves to the
  **guarantor's** salary:
  - **Loans** — the guarantor is named by the requester and must consent in
    their ESS portal (My Self-Service → My Loans) before the approval
    workflow starts (unchanged consent flow, spec §10.1).
  - **Advances** — HR designates the guarantor directly on the create form
    ("Guarantor (fallback payer)"); the designation is effective immediately.
- The guarantor never loses more than a configurable **% of their net pay per
  period, cumulative across every loan/advance they guarantee**; the
  remainder of the installment is **deferred** (surfaced as a warning, never
  silently dropped).

**The control system:** Payroll → **Loan & Advance Control** (new tab in the
"Recovery Control" group):
- Switches: loan recovery on/off, advance recovery on/off, **guarantor
  fallback on/off**, and the **guarantor deduction cap (% of net)**.
- **Recovery Plan** for the selected month — one row per due installment:
  who pays (borrower / **guarantor** / deferred), the borrower's presence,
  the guarantor, and the **exact amount this period**.
- **Guarantor Exposure** — every guarantor, how many loans/advances they
  guarantee, and the total outstanding they are liable for.

**Ledger timing (as requested):** the deduction is *shown* at calculation
(exact amounts on entries + payslip breakdowns) but **lands on the
loan/advance ledgers only at FINAL APPROVAL (pay)** — the pay step recovers
the borrower's installment/advance rows, marks them paid, closes loans whose
last installment is recovered, posts the accounting journal, and records an
exact reversal record so a cancelled/re-created month never double-recovers.

**Database:** migration `10_loan_guarantor_control_20261014` adds
`Advance.guarantorId/guarantorStatus` and
`PayrollEntry.guarantorDeduction(+Paisa)`. Nullable + backfill-free — existing
rows simply have no guarantor and keep the historical behaviour.

## 3) Advisory — are "Field Force" and "Field & Security" the same thing?

**No — they are different modules that share one GPS data stream:**

| | **Field Force** (Operations) | **Field & Security** (Field & Devices) |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Field **visit management** — visit locations, daily route plans, GPS-validated check-ins at stops, visit compliance reports, the Location Hit Map | Attendance **security** — live location tracking, geo-fence punch validation, attendance security events |
| Primary users | Sales/operations managers planning and auditing field visits | HR/security admins protecting attendance integrity |
| Data | Visit plans, stops, check-ins + the same TrackingPoints | Geo-fences, security events + the same TrackingPoints |

**Recommendation: keep them separate.** They serve different roles, workflows
and permissions; merging would bury visit planning inside a security screen.
The r7 menu restructure already places them in adjacent groups with
unambiguous labels. If your users later report confusion, a low-cost option is
a single "Field Operations" top-level menu with two tab groups — both views
are tab-based, so that merge is a small change. A cross-link (from the Hit Map
to Live Tracking and back) would also bridge them without merging.

  loan / advance / **guarantor** deduction amounts (columns + the breakdown
  dialog, which itemises "Guarantor recovery — <borrower> (loan/advance)").
- **Final approval → pay**: the borrower's loan installment / advance
  outstanding is recovered, ledgers and journals post, payslips itemise the
  lines, and the exact recovery is recorded for precise reversal if the run
  is ever cancelled.
